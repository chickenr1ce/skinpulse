    Description

    With its curved blade mimicking a tiger's claw, the karambit was developed as part of the southeast Asian martial discipline of silat. The knife is typically used with a reverse grip, with the finger ring on the index finger. It has been stonewashed and given a black laminate handle.

    History

    The ★ Karambit | Black Laminate was first introduced to CS2 9 years ago, on June 15th, 2016. It was released as part of the Gamma 2 Case, alongside the "Gamma Exposure" update.

    Origin

    The ★ Karambit | Black Laminate can be obtained by opening a Gamma 2 Case or Gamma Case container. The skin is not part of any collections.

    Popularity

    With a popularity of 35%, the ★ Karambit | Black Laminate is currently one of the less popular items in CS2. This popularity measurement is based on the daily sales volume and the price of the skin.

    Rarity

    The ★ Karambit | Black Laminate is one of 604 other Knife skins. The rarity of the skin is Covert, which makes the Black Laminate an ultra rare drop with an estimated drop chance of just 0.26%.

    Availability

    With a price range between $660.31 and $1,660.49, the ★ Karambit | Black Laminate is an extremely expensive skin. Fortunately, it's currently widely available and can be purchased on multiple different markets.

    Versions

    The float value of the ★ Karambit | Black Laminate ranges from 0.00 to 1.00, which makes the skin available in all exteriors. For each exterior, there also exists a StatTrak version for the Black Laminate.

    Finish Style

    This Karambit has been painted in a "Gunsmith" style, with the Black Laminate finish. This style uses a combination of patina and custom paint styles. The skin has been painted in a color scheme of black and gray. Different pattern indexes have no effect on the Black Laminate finish. The skin uses the old legacy model.

    The ★ Karambit | Black Laminate has received over 5.7K votes on CSGOSKINS.GG, leading to an average rating of 4.9 out of 5 stars. The skin is currently also owned by the professional CS2 players steel, jame and reck.
